*----------------------------------------------------------------------*

*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
AT SELECTION-SCREEN ON VALUE-REQUEST FOR P_DIR.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
  PERFORM PRO_REQUEST_DIR.

*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
AT SELECTION-SCREEN.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
  CASE SSCRFIELDS-UCOMM.
    WHEN 'FC01'.  "템플릿다운
      PERFORM EXCEL_DOWN.
  ENDCASE.

*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
START-OF-SELECTION.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
  IF P_CHECK = 'X'.
    PERFORM PRO_REQUEST_DIR.
  ENDIF.

  IF P_DIR IS NOT INITIAL.
    PERFORM GET_TABLE_DEF.

    IF P_EXCEL = 'X'.
      PERFORM EXCEL_UPLOAD_RTN.
    ELSE.
      PERFORM TEXT_UPLOAD_RTN.
    ENDIF.

    PERFORM CREATE_BDC_DATA.
  ELSE.
    MESSAGE I000 WITH TEXT-I02.
  ENDIF.

*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
END-OF-SELECTION.
