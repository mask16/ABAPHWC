    "체크1 : 문제가 되는 특수문자가 캐리지 리턴 문자 인지

    REPLACE ALL OCCURRENCES OF  CL_ABAP_CHAR_UTILITIES=>HORIZONTAL_TAB

    REPLACE ALL OCCURRENCES OF  CL_ABAP_CHAR_UTILITIES=>NEWLINE IN

    REPLACE ALL OCCURRENCES OF  CL_ABAP_CHAR_UTILITIES=>VERTICAL_TAB
IN pl_in WITH ' '.

    "체크5 : 문제가 되는 특수문자가 폼피드 문자 인지 구분
    REPLACE ALL OCCURRENCES OF  CL_ABAP_CHAR_UTILITIES=>FORM_FEED IN
pl_in WITH ' '.

    REPLACE ALL OCCURRENCES OF REGEX
