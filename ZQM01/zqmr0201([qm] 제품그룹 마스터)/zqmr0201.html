*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
*INITIALIZATION.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
INITIALIZATION.
  PERFORM SET_INITIALIZATION.
  PERFORM ALV_VAR_INITIAL.
  PERFORM GET_DEFAULT_VARIANT.

*--------------------------------------------------------------------*
* AT SELECTION-SCREEN
*--------------------------------------------------------------------*
AT SELECTION-SCREEN.
  GV_OK_CODE = SY-UCOMM.
  PERFORM AT_SELECTION_SCREEN.


*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
*AT SELECTION-SCREEN.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
*  LAY OUT 변형 설정
AT SELECTION-SCREEN ON VALUE-REQUEST FOR P_VARI.
  PERFORM F4_FOR_VARIANT CHANGING P_VARI.


*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
* AT SELECTION-SCREEN ON
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
AT SELECTION-SCREEN ON P_VARI.
  PERFORM CHECK_VARIANT USING P_VARI.


*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
*START-OF-SELECTION.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
START-OF-SELECTION.
  PERFORM GET_LIST_DATA.

  IF P_R1 = C_X.
    CLEAR GV_DATA_CNT.
    GV_DATA_CNT = LINES( GT_DATA ).
    IF GV_DATA_CNT EQ C_0.
      MESSAGE S010 WITH TEXT-M01 DISPLAY LIKE C_E.
      EXIT.
    ELSE.
      PERFORM MOVE_LIST_DATA.
*-- Screen 100호출
      START=>S100( ).
    ENDIF.
  ELSEIF P_R2 = C_X.
    PERFORM MOVE_LIST_DATA.
    START=>S100( ).
  ENDIF.


*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
*END-OF-SELECTION.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
END-OF-SELECTION.
