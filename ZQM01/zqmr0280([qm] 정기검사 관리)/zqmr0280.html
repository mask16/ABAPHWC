*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
AT SELECTION-SCREEN ON P_VARI.

*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
*START-OF-SELECTION.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
START-OF-SELECTION.
  PERFORM INI_DATA.

  IF P_R1 = C_X. "검사의뢰서
    PERFORM GET_LIST_DATA.
    PERFORM MOVE_LIST_DATA.
*-- Screen 100호출
    START=>S100( ).

  ELSEIF P_R2 = C_X. "정기검사관리
    PERFORM GET_DATA.
    PERFORM MODIFY_DATA.
*-- Screen 200호출
    START=>S300( ).
  ENDIF.

*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
*END-OF-SELECTION.
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
END-OF-SELECTION.
